## 0 · Headline

A single structural decision determines whether ~₹50–100 L of non-dilutive money + 70%+ compute discounts are accessible. The decision: **incorporate Age Ayurveda Companion Pvt Ltd as a sister entity to the existing manufacturing company — not as a subsidiary.** Once that's done, the rest is a 90-day stacking exercise across five non-overlapping levers.

The single highest-asymmetry move on the page is **a research partnership letter to CCRAS (Central Council for Research in Ayurvedic Sciences)** — exactly the body whose mandate is to validate and digitise the classical Ayurvedic corpus this platform already retrieves from. Walking that letter in via 100-year heritage credentials is leverage no other AI startup in India can replicate.

## 1 · The structural decision (Week 1)

### Decision matrix

| Option | DPIIT eligible | Sec 80-IAC | FFS access | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keep AI work inside the manufacturing entity | No (>10 yr old) | No | No | Cheapest. Forfeits ~₹50 L+. |
| Wholly-owned subsidiary of the manufacturer | **No** (DPIIT bars subsidiaries) | No | No | **Avoid.** |
| **Sister Pvt Ltd, founders as direct shareholders** | **Yes** | **Yes** | **Yes** | **The play.** |

### Rule that drives this
- DPIIT G.S.R. 127(E) §2(d) — disqualifies entities formed by "splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business" AND holding/subsidiary structures.
- Therefore: shareholders of the new Pvt Ltd must be **individuals** (the founder generation), not the manufacturing company.

### Compliance pre-work (CA + company secretary)
- [ ] **CA opinion** on whether AI work already booked inside the manufacturing company creates a "splitting up" risk. Defensible if pre-revenue / exploratory; risky if AI revenue or capitalised assets exist.
- [ ] Draft minimal IP-licence + services agreement template for arm's-length transactions between the two entities. Distinct customers. No asset spin-out.
- [ ] Reserve company name (MCA RUN form) — propose: "Age Ayurveda Companion Pvt Ltd" or similar.

### Incorporation
- [ ] SPICe+ via MCA portal (mca.gov.in). MoA + AoA. Two directors minimum, two shareholders minimum.
- [ ] PAN + TAN auto-generated.
- [ ] Bank account opening (ICICI / HDFC / SBI — same banker as the manufacturing entity is fine).
- [ ] GST registration (mandatory if cross-state services or B2B SaaS to corporates — register day-one).

**Cost:** ₹15–25 K incorporation + ₹40–60 K/yr ongoing compliance (ROC + audit). Trivial relative to what it unlocks.

## 2 · Foundational registrations (Weeks 2–3)

These are gateway — every Tier-1 and Tier-2 lever downstream depends on at least one.

### 2.1 Udyam (MSME)
- **Where:** udyamregistration.gov.in
- **Cost:** Free
- **Time:** Same day
- **Filing:** NIC codes **62011** (custom software development), **62013** (software support), **62091** (other IT services). All three on one Udyam.
- **Classification:** Micro / Service (turnover ≤ ₹10 Cr; investment in plant ≤ ₹2.5 Cr — both satisfied trivially).
- **Unlocks:** CGTMSE collateral-free loan ceiling (₹10 Cr post-2025), 45-day buyer-payment rule, GeM Startup Runway eligibility, IndiaAI MSME-track compute, UP MSME-2022 perks.

### 2.2 DPIIT Startup India recognition
- **Where:** startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/registration.html
- **Cost:** Free
- **Time:** 2–10 working days
- **Filing requirements:** CIN + PAN + MoA + brief innovation/scalability statement (3–5 paragraphs). Frame Companion as "novel application of LLM-grounded retrieval to classical Sanskrit Ayurvedic texts — first such platform globally."
- **Unlocks:** Sec 80-IAC, FFS pipeline, IndiaAI startup-track compute, UP StartinUP grants, GeM Startup Runway, self-certification for labour/environment compliance.

### 2.3 Sec 80-IAC (tax holiday — apply immediately, takes longest)
- **Where:** startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/form80iac.html (post-DPIIT)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Time:** ~120-day decision (Inter-Ministerial Board)
- **Benefit:** 100% profit deduction for any 3 consecutive years out of first 10. Extended by Budget 2025 to entities incorporated **before 1 April 2030**.
- **Application kit:** DPIIT certificate, MoA, audited financials (year 1 = nil + projections), pitch deck (use the platform pitch), patent/IP filings if any.

### 2.4 GST registration
- **Where:** gst.gov.in
- **Cost:** Free
- **Time:** 7 working days
- **Why now:** Required for B2B SaaS invoicing the moment revenue starts.

### 2.5 Bank current account + payment-gateway
- ICICI Privilege Banking / HDFC Smartup are the two most startup-friendly. Open before any revenue.
- Razorpay / Stripe India for SaaS billing.

## 7 · Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| "Splitting up" doctrine bites at DPIIT review | CA paper-trail of pre-incorporation AI work as exploratory + non-revenue. No asset spin-out from manufacturing entity. |
| EMR grant rejected for lacking academic credentials | Lock co-PI early. Frame as research instrument, not product. |
| IndiaAI End-User Policy disallows foreign LLM API use | Workload separation: use IndiaAI compute only for embeddings + fine-tuning; Anthropic API stays as the chat-time inference layer. |
| SAMRIDH cohort doesn't open soon enough | TIDE 2.0 fallback (₹25 L) via IIIT-D / IIM-A Ventures. |
| 80-IAC profit deduction unusable in Y1–Y2 (no profits yet) | Defer claim to Y3–Y5 — the rule allows any 3 consecutive years out of first 10. |
| UP StartinUP processing delays | Apply concurrently with DPIIT; processing tends to lag 60–120 days. |
| CCRAS letter goes unanswered | Follow-up via Baidyanath group's existing AYUSH ministry contacts; co-sign with manufacturing-entity letterhead for institutional weight. |
