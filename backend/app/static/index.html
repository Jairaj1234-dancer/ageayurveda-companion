The Science of Life

Ayurveda — आयुर्वेद — is a 5,000-year-old holistic healing system. It teaches that true health arises when body, mind, and spirit exist in harmony with nature's rhythms.

Every person carries a unique blend of three vital energies — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha. Understanding your constitution is the first step toward lasting wellness.

The Three Pillars

Discover Your Dosha

💨

Vata

Air + Space

Creative, quick-thinking, energetic. Thin frame, dry skin, cold extremities. Thrives on warm, grounding routines and nourishing foods.

🔥

Pitta

Fire + Water

Sharp intellect, strong digestion, natural leader. Medium build, warm body. Thrives on cooling foods, moderate exercise, and calm environments.

🌊

Kapha

Earth + Water

Steady, compassionate, enduring. Larger frame, smooth skin. Thrives on light, warm, stimulating foods and vigorous daily movement.
